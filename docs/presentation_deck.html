The Problem & Core Innovation

Overcoming registration friction and black-box search limitations

Traditional Challenges

  • Mandatory signups prevent quick reporting by stressed users.
  • Keyword searches miss typos, abbreviations, and synonyms.
  • Black-box AI models fail to explain why two items matched.
  • Lack of item retrieval tracking and verified handover lifecycle.

Our Solution Architecture

  • Frictionless Reporting: 30-sec form without mandatory account creation.
  • Explainable Scoring: Deterministic weights across 7 structured attributes.
  • Instant Match & Explain Modal: Real-time score breakdown.
  • Retrieval Archive: Admin-controlled "Collected & Retrieved" state.
